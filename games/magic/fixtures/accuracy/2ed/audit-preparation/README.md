# Unlimited audit preparation

This is an unfinished audit after the Alpha/Beta 0.5.4 release. It does not certify
Unlimited, change renderer layout defaults, or contain redistributed artwork.
The older `annotations.json` is historical partial evidence; the new scan-bound
`annotations-printed-types`, `annotations-empty-rules`, `annotations-printed-fields`
and `annotations-text-outliers` files define the additional reviewed fields.

Every one of the 302 type and credit/stat/cost strips was inspected. Eighteen
flavor-only panels explicitly supply empty original rules; other rules retain
their stated source rather than silently importing Oracle. Artist spelling is
printing-specific: Icy Manipulator and Mountain 299 print Shuler, while the other
reviewed Schuler credits retain that spelling. Reviewed corrections use the same
public resolver as consuming applications.

Frame-only normalization accepted all 302 references. Its three strip groups
separate fitting, model selection and certification; replacing interior content
leaves their profiles unchanged. The weakest certificate in each of seven
materials was visually inspected. Six known-transform controls on real white,
black and artifact references recover control points within 0.15 canonical pixels.
These are comparison transforms, never renderer defaults. A completion count alone
does not certify native geometry, wording or pigment.

The experimental count/wrap transfer improves the rules objective by 12.48/100
on 201 calibration printings and 12.30/100 on 101 validation printings. These cohorts
are reused development data, and these are **regional fitting indices**, not
whole-card accuracy. All 302 candidates passed native replay and region isolation.
Five improvements and five regressions were inspected. Dense passages and some
medium wrapping regress; the transfer is **not adopted**. Frame/source selection,
full typography, mana, pigment and integrated validation still need work.

Compressed inputs, poses and study observations retain the exact hashes needed
to restore cached assets. `review.json` lists their integrity hashes, pinned
components, method identity, inspected examples and remaining tasks. Native
captures used the 0.5.4 painter with these unreleased printing corrections; they
must not be presented as immutable published 0.5.4 output. Provenance-only metadata
may subsequently change without changing the frozen face recipes or raster pixels.

Use `calibration-study.mjs init 2ed` after `npm run magic:data:build` to regenerate
reviewed draft faces, `abu-calibration-normalize.mjs` for frame-only registration,
and `abu-type-study.mjs` for the recorded count/wrap trial. Each tool lists its CLI
arguments. Keep originals, name-grouped cohorts, source assets and poses frozen;
do not select candidates against an easier replacement target.
