# card-glint 0.2.10

The optional Magic pack now reproduces more historical typography, symbols and
frame geometry, with explicit original/Oracle wording and symbol-era choices.
The game-neutral core and original showcase remain independent.

- Improve early-set titles, credits, rules margins, centering, mana placement,
  original sun/tap symbols and printed-detail handling. Hosts still supply text;
  `withCardWording` selects wording, alignment source and symbol style separately.
- Refine ordinary modern apertures, type/rules borders, dual-land palettes,
  flavor dividers, planeswalker text, badges, shadows and lower panel curves.
- Add measured historical set-symbol treatments and visible-bound sizing,
  including shared public/direct loading of opaque white symbol details.
- Add opt-in `colorCalibration: 'set-hsb-v1'` for admitted old-frame set, border
  and material profiles. One transform covers the frame and rules paper, with
  separate mana targets. Rejected/sparse groups retain source colors; omitting
  the option preserves the default color behavior.
- Reuse derived color bitmaps in a bounded 16 MiB pack cache, with whole-face
  admission, shared builds, cancellation handling and lease-safe teardown.
- Record frame-family comparison and HSB coverage, source/method provenance,
  reserved measurements and full-card raster checks in the Magic pack. Structural
  scores exclude artwork and do not claim whole-card accuracy percentages.

The review browser, downloaded card scans and artwork remain outside the release.
External source references and fan-content terms are in the
[Magic pack](../../games/magic/README.md).
