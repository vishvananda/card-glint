# card-glint 0.5.1

This release is being assembled as a batch of calibration fixes.

Alpha rules and flavor text retain their full filled strokes, and names/types
use darker solid gray. An older Alpha-only perimeter-thinning adjustment made
body text too light. Font sizes, wrapping, placement, shadows and footer ink
remain unchanged. Public interfaces are unchanged.

The correction was checked on 38 independently aligned printing scans with
frozen frame poses, plus 54 pixel-identical non-Alpha controls. Original
calibration/validation membership was retained. Two additional scans with
rejected frame alignments remain excluded from the ink fit.

On 18 repeated-validation cards, the middle-threshold filled-ink area relative
to the scans improves from 0.56 to 0.96. Mean whole-card structural similarity
changes from 0.6080 to 0.6117. These are different diagnostics, not percentages
of correctness. Edge overlap alone substantially understates the visible
stroke-weight difference. Heading gray was selected visually across six frame
materials, independently of color-insensitive edge scores.

This is an ink correction, not approval of Alpha's complete typography.
Geometry, font-shape differences and original type-line coverage still need
review. Unverified Oracle type fallbacks remain excluded from type scores.

The Dark's Electric Eel restores the printed activation colon and paragraph
break in original wording. The correction is tied to its reviewed scan;
Oracle wording remains unchanged.
