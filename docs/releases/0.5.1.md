# card-glint 0.5.1

This release is being assembled as a batch of calibration fixes.

Alpha rules and flavor text retain their full filled strokes, and names/types
use darker solid gray. An older Alpha-only perimeter-thinning adjustment made
body text too light. Font sizes, wrapping, placement, shadows and footer ink
remain unchanged. Public interfaces are unchanged.

The correction was checked on 38 independently aligned printing scans with
frozen frame poses, plus 54 pixel-identical non-Alpha controls. Original
calibration/validation membership was retained. Two additional scans with
rejected frame alignments remain excluded from the ink fit.

On 18 repeated-validation cards, the middle-threshold filled-ink area relative
to the scans improves from 0.56 to 0.96. Mean whole-card structural similarity
changes from 0.6080 to 0.6117. These are different diagnostics, not percentages
of correctness. Edge overlap alone substantially understates the visible
stroke-weight difference. Heading gray was selected visually across six frame
materials, independently of color-insensitive edge scores.

This is an ink correction, not approval of Alpha's complete typography.
Geometry, font-shape differences and original type-line coverage still need
review. Unverified Oracle type fallbacks remain excluded from type scores.

The Dark's Electric Eel restores the printed activation colon and paragraph
break in original wording. The correction is tied to its reviewed scan;
Oracle wording remains unchanged.

The Dark also gets a shared type-line size, position, width and filled-gray
ink adjustment. The candidate matches all 122 native prototypes, while 194
Arabian Nights/Antiquities renders and 84 cross-set controls remain identical.
The 88 repeated-validation cards improve mean whole-card structural similarity
from 0.7741 to 0.7792. Ball Lightning's rejected alignment stays unscored;
the shifted Gaea's Touch printing and Wormwood Treefolk's small fine-stroke
regression remain recorded outliers.

The Dark's body text now uses separate shared margins and line spacing for
short regular, long regular and compact text. The existing size-class selector
and large centered layout are preserved. An earlier candidate that promoted
compact text into regular type was rejected. Goblin Wizard and Ghost Ship also
restore activation colons in their reviewed original wording; Oracle wording
is unchanged.

All 121 normalized native body renders match their fitted prototypes, with
zero changed pixels outside the body. The 194 other early-set cards and 84
cross-set controls remain identical. On 87 repeated-validation printings,
text overlap improves from 0.6088 to 0.7072 and whole-card structural similarity
from 0.7794 to 0.8135. Full-card scores improve on 64, remain unchanged on 16,
and regress on 7. The largest remaining text regressions are Giant Shark,
Runesword and People of the Woods; they remain visible in the calibration
ledger. Body typography stays partial rather than approved for the whole set.

Both versions use identical corrected original wording for comparisons.
Full-card scores use canonical 488×680 images and frozen frame alignment.
The report's broad frame regions overlap some body text, so those diagnostic
scores can change even though the frame pixels themselves are unchanged.
