# card-glint 0.3.8

The Magic pack now renders and calibrates Planar Chaos's ordinary and
colorshifted frames. Set `frameAssetFamily: 'eighth'` and retain
`frameTreatment: 'colorshifted'` when displaying a colorshifted printing with
Oracle wording. The treatment selects its physical frame, white shadowed
lettering and matching power/toughness badge.

Shared measured defaults cover inner-frame geometry, title/type typography,
mana and expansion-symbol placement, rules spacing, artist credit and the
2007 copyright/collector line. MSE's ModMatrix font improves P/T digit spacing.
Two-color gold cards now use the source pack's colored pinline mask. Printing
colors remain opt-in with `set-hsb-v1`; source-bound masks separate outer
material, rules paper and title/type panels.

All 165 printings render, including 45 colorshifted cards. On 156 accepted,
independently normalized scans, the structural similarity index improves from
**64.6% to 81.3%**, with 155 cards improving. Six scans fail the frozen alignment
checks; three split cards have no validated normalization in this study.
The index includes lettering, symbols, frames, art and credits, with corner
regions and a separate whole-image diagnostic. Color is measured separately.
It is not a percentage of correct pixels, and differs from the live review
application's current region map.

Rules spacing preserves existing font sizes and line breaks after a font/reflow
trial regressed several cards. Repeated validation improves 96/123 rules
regions, 123/123 artist/legal regions and 84/84 P/T regions against the initial
imported defaults. Text size/wrapping, variable-stat stars, badge shapes and
isolated printing-color differences remain in the review queue. Heroes
Remembered is the one whole-card structural regression and stays flagged.

The changes are additive and do not break existing consumer interfaces.
Reviewed original type corrections restore the printed Lord subtype for
Crovax, Benalish Commander and Jedit Ojanen, and remove Timbermare's later Horse
subtype. They are available through `resolvePrintingWording`; Oracle types stay
unchanged.
Assets and derived materials are prepared through existing caches; animation
does not reload fonts, decode images or read back masks.
