# card-glint 0.2.16

Render Sagas with native frame plates and measured chapter typography.

- Use pinned textured Saga frames, chapter badges, dividers and stamp assets.
- Measure chapter text before distributing space and shrinking dense rules.
- Keep grouped chapter numerals together and preserve inline reminder italics.
- Support transforming Saga fronts, including their indicator and optional
  reverse-face power/toughness reminder.
- Cache added fonts and decoded decorations during face preparation.
- Validate comparisons with frame-only scan normalization and retain rejected
  fits and text regressions in the calibration evidence.

Across 17 accepted fits from a 20-face sample, mean dark-letter edge overlap
improves from .356 to .800 for names, .239 to .758 for reminders, .339 to .494
for chapters, and .687 to .888 for type lines. These sampled measurements are
not whole-card accuracy percentages. Three scans remain unscored; chapter
spacing outliers and missing source paper motifs remain documented.

Strict checks, build, package integration, browser, original showcase, Magic
layouts and accuracy regression suites pass. The branch CI also passes.
