# card-glint 0.6.6

Alpha's plain original rules text keeps its existing line wrapping when applying
the calibrated inset. The safeguard uses the established wider allowance only
when it removes an extra line; font size and mixed/flavor passages retain their
existing policy. There are no printing-name or scan-offset lookups in this rule.

A paired native replay covers all 295 Alpha printings. Four passages improve:
Drain Life, Cyclopean Tomb, Keldon Warlord and Nether Shadow. Their mean regional
text index rises from 52.85 to 59.49/100; the other 291 faces are byte-identical.
These regional indices are not whole-card scores or percentages of correctness.

Beta Tundra uses the reviewed white outer outline and Beta Volcanic Island uses
blue. All ten Beta duals match the reviewed native candidate exactly: two change
and eight retain their prior pixels. Alpha and Unlimited retain their own palettes.

The public original-wording resolver adds 230 exact-printing Unlimited type
reviews and Wall of Ice's explicitly empty printed rules field. Oracle wording
remains separate. Every other wording correction and all 182 artist reviews
retain their values. The 230 proposed type labels were checked against their own
Unlimited scans before adoption.

Face-cache revisions are scoped to Alpha originals and the two Beta outlines.
All 597 Alpha/Beta etching-mask keys remain unchanged. Reversible source receipts
retain existing historical evidence and reopen only changed calibration
components; they do not approve an old fit for a changed component. Whole-set
geometry and outlier reviews continue through Fourth Edition.

This is a patch release with no consumer API changes. Experimental reconstructed
Alpha frames remain preview-only. Reference images are cached outside the public
showcase; the repository contains numerical evidence and raster hashes.
