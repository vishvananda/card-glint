# card-glint 0.3.3

The public showcase now presents eight treatments on four original cards in two
groups: prismatic, cold, Opal and etched; then rain, pool, heat and gloss. Both
groups have new eight-second GIFs and MP4s. Dawnwing Griffin joins the original
art collection, with its prompt and provenance included. The four-card layout
adapts to desktop, tablet and phone widths. Switching groups reuses prepared
faces and masks.

The Magic pack fixes Revised power/toughness: solid light-gray ink replaces the
hollow edge treatment, with the existing lower-right shadow. A shared fit raises
the font from 26 to 27.25 px and moves it up 1 px, keeping the right anchor fixed.
On 12 held-out creatures, local edge overlap improves from 51.38% to 55.38% versus
0.3.2. All 24 production renders reproduce the fitting measurements. Scan poses
remain frozen; there are no per-card stat-placement overrides.

Sixth Edition uncommon and rare expansion symbols now have white outer rims.
Their shared placement moves 2 px right and 2 px up and grows 8% in each axis.
With the corrected rim held fixed, mean symbol overlap on six held-out cards
improves from 73.94% to 78.69%; all six improve. Ebon Stronghold retains a
shape/material warning, and one rejected scan remains excluded. These figures
are structural overlap indices, not percentages of correct pixels.

Mirage, Tempest and Sixth Edition automatically right-align final dash-prefixed
flavor attributions, including wrapped names. Explicit paragraph alignment and
centered passages take precedence. Revised and M21 scans demonstrate that this
is not a universal rule, so unreviewed sets retain their previous defaults.
The rules-centering control now preserves explicit flavor-paragraph alignment.

Public interfaces are unchanged. This is a patch release; existing applications
do not need to change their inputs.
