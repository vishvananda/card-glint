# card-glint 0.4.17

The Magic pack now uses matching frame sources for The Dark's four nonbasic
lands and three multicolor cards. The lands use an ABU plate with independently
corrected paper and dark outlines; the gold cards use a Fourth Edition plate.
Shared source geometry, artwork apertures and component colors replace the
previous Seventh Edition fallback. Public interfaces are unchanged.

Scan-only scale, rotation and skew normalization precedes source selection.
The original fitting cohorts remain fixed: two training and two reserved land
names, and one training and two reserved gold names. On the reserved cards,
frame-boundary correlation improves from 0.103 to 0.875 for lands and from 0.090
to 0.952 for gold. Frame color distance falls from 9.61 to 1.27 and from 8.51 to
2.75 respectively (Oklab × 100, lower is better). These are regional diagnostics,
not percentages of whole-card correctness or a basis for transferring the
profiles to other sets.

The native replay matches all seven reviewed prototypes exactly, preserves all
309 other early-expansion faces and leaves 84 unrelated printing controls
unchanged. Existing calibration receipts retain their original measurements;
a checked source reconstruction preserves their ancestry. The coverage ledger
records frame/color progress while keeping typography, symbols, the thin land
outline and complete-set certification open.

[Numeric evidence](../../games/magic/fixtures/accuracy/early-expansion-dark-frames.json)
includes the frozen normalization, independent checks, component results and
native compatibility records. External scan images are not distributed.
