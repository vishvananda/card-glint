# card-glint 0.6.3

Standard planeswalkers use Card Conjurer background textures with shared HSB
corrections for all seven palettes, across regular and tall frames. The existing
frame, artwork, rules layout, typography, badges and title/type paper remain.

- Across 59 accepted nonfoil scans, mean background channel error improves from
  36.71 to 17.06/255. Every palette improves. These are regional RGB errors;
  lower is better. They are not whole-card compliance scores.
- The type-box edit corrects its upper-right quadratic curve and clips unwanted
  rightward shadow at the rim. The
  previous type slot, left flare, bottom contour, paper and down-left shadow
  remain. Text and expansion-symbol anchors do not move.
- An earlier complete type-plate replacement was rejected after enlarged visual
  review, despite lower paper-color error. It is not shipped. Its evidence is
  retained to show why color agreement does not certify contours or shadows.
- Fits use 39 printings, with 20 reserved from parameter fitting. Original
  MTGJSON wording is used. Exterior affine registration includes translation,
  scale, rotation and shear, and excludes the fitted type region. Two failed
  registrations and one printing lacking original wording remain explicit.
- Backgrounds are prepared once under a separate 64 MiB cache. Warm preparation
  shares leases, document and worker outputs match, and missing sources fail
  before painting a partial face. No processing runs during animated frames.

This is a compatible patch with no required consumer API changes. Non-planeswalker
face keys stay identical. Changed planeswalkers require fresh accuracy scores;
historical evidence keeps its original identity. Reference images and external
frame pixels are not distributed with the library or public showcase.

Sources, native regional errors, rounding-fringe counts, and corner measurements
are under `games/magic/fixtures/accuracy/planeswalker-materials/`. The reconstruction
tool README documents reproduction and the rejected type-plate experiment.
