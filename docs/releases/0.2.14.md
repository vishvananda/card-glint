# card-glint 0.2.14

Correct the metallic Eighth and Ninth Edition set symbols in the Magic pack.

- Eighth Edition uncommon and rare retain metallic faces on all three cards,
  with a black frame and numeral on the center card.
- Ninth Edition uncommon and rare use a black numeral with a silver/gold
  outline. The side panels and common versions are unchanged.
- Invalidate cached card faces and verify both rarities with pixel checks,
  including source-cache reuse and unchanged common symbols.

The alignment follow-up expands four old-frame cohorts to 212 nonfoil cards,
with 172 accepted frame normalizations. A smaller, frozen symbol region now
checks dark ink and light outlines separately from the broad comparison.
Seventh Edition and Urza's Saga candidates improve average overlap but regress
on individual reserved cards, so no new placement defaults are adopted.
Legacy and Destiny retain their existing placements after no reserved gain.

Strict Rust/TypeScript/format checks, build, package integration, browser,
original showcase and Magic regression checks pass. These are sampled component
scores, not an accuracy claim for every card.
