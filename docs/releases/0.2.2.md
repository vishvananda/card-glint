Moving heat silhouettes, recessed rain artwork, and projected pool caustics.

- Full-card heat now bends the rounded card outline over the background. Rust
  picking/culling, card-local overlays and masks follow the same deformation.
  Artwork-only heat keeps the frame fixed. Warped PNGs include transparent margins.
- Rain places the image behind the drops, with tilt-driven parallax. Pool places
  it deeper beneath the wave surface. Artwork-only sampling stays inside the art.
- Pool light is projected through a wave mesh onto a separate floor map. Focused
  rays brighten and diverging rays darken the floor in linear light; surface
  reflections and ripples sit above it.
- All pool cards share one light pass per changed time and reuse the map while
  paused. Cached faces/edges stay intact, with no animation-time texture uploads
  or readbacks. The fixed light-map/index allocation is 688 KiB per context.

The new optional `Instance.deformation` field is backward compatible; ordinary
instances keep fixed outlines. See the [API and architecture](../architecture.md)
and [optical references and limits](../optical-surfaces.md).
