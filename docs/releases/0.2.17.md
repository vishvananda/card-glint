# card-glint 0.2.17

Render Aftermath and spell-split cards with dedicated frame plates, and restore
the paired-color wraps on two-color gold Sagas.

- Add native Aftermath geometry with consistent art, printing and foil apertures.
- Add old-border, 2003 and M15 split plates, including connected Fuse bars and
  one shared reminder strip.
- Separate M15 hybrid label paper from gold panels and preserve colored pinlines.
- Keep per-panel wording, art crops, watermarks and artist credits in the face
  cache key; bound the larger prepared M15 materials to 16 MiB.
- Normalize cached scans against frame rails before scoring text. Exclude the
  modern authenticity stamp, and retain rejected fits without scores.

The studies include 25 Aftermath cards, 35 old/2003 split cards, 25 modern
split/Fuse printings and 20 two-color Kaldheim Sagas. Modern typography transfers
to reserved RNA and DMC/MOC cohorts, with mixed RNA right-panel results. The
comparison receipts explicitly retain wrapping regressions, failed frame fits
and source-asset gaps. These are component measurements, not whole-card accuracy
percentages. Rooms, Adventure refinements, Prepared, Battles and physical DFC
variants remain separate work.

Strict checks, build, independent package integration, browser, original
showcase, Magic layouts and accuracy regressions pass locally. All 35 earlier
split captures remain pixel-identical after the M15 addition. Publication is
gated by the full release CI.
