# card-glint 0.3.10

Planar Chaos P/T badges now match the scanned placement and proportions more
closely. Ordinary and colorshifted badges have separate shared fits. Variable
P/T stars use smaller ink while preserving their advances, slash and surrounding
numeric typography. Existing Conjurer assets remain the selected sources after
comparison with MSE alternatives.

The text-fit clearance is independent of badge geometry. This preserves rules
font sizes, line breaks and pixels on all 165 printings; a coupled trial that
reflowed 22 cards was rejected.

Across 156 accepted, independently normalized scans, whole-card structural
similarity rises from **81.34 to 82.45 / 100** against 0.3.9. All 105 scored P/T
cards improve, and the other 51 are unchanged. The P/T-region score rises from
76.33 to 96.08 / 100. Badge fitting used 21 calibration cards and 84 reused
validation cards. Six rejected single-face scans and three split cards remain
explicit exclusions. These are structural similarity indices, not percentages
of correct pixels; rules-text size and wrapping outliers remain open.

This patch has no public interface changes. It retains the cardstock relief and
cached print-dot optimizations, and introduces no per-frame work. Full replay,
package/browser/showcase checks, strict Clippy, formatting and targeted asset
and printing-color checks validate the change. Versioned score and source
identities are included in the Magic pack's accuracy fixtures.
