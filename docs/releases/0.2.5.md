# card-glint 0.2.5

Over-finish printing can now opt into a subtle gold, silver or prismatic satin
reflection with `FaceRecipe.overlayFoil`. Its strength is adjustable from 0–1.
The reflection follows tilt while the printing remains sharp above animated
rain, heat and water. Existing unshaded overlays keep their behavior.

Tone and strength are uniforms in the existing depth pass. Changing them reuses
cached artwork, printing and masks; exports use the same composition. Browser
checks cover sharp printing, changing time/tilt, validation and cache reuse.
