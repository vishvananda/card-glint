# card-glint 0.2.12

Old-frame printing colors can now use separate frame and paper adjustments.
Source masks follow the irregular green and black paper edges without moving
embedded shadows. Other colors use fixed paper interiors. Recolored bitmaps
remain shared across cards and tiers in the bounded material cache.

- Correct the pink cast on Urza's Saga white and the excess contrast and brown
  shadows on Urza's Destiny white. Recolor frame-texture highlights with their
  material, removing the false embossed effect caused by exempting white pixels.
- Remove unintended modern drop shadows from retro cost symbols.
- Audit 2,898 cached examples from 62 sets with frame normalization and separate
  chroma, shadow and texture-contrast checks. Ship 53 frame/paper profiles across
  32 sets, including six visually reviewed Revised profiles, and 151 mana targets.
- Retire combined frame/paper fits. Rejected groups use source colors and report
  absent coverage; sparse normalization and remaining asset differences are
  recorded explicitly. This reduces automatic coverage rather than retaining
  coefficients that failed the new checks.

One reserved full-card render per active profile reduces median sampled color
error from 6.22 to 1.00 relative to unadjusted assets, with unchanged art interiors.
Revised Stone Giant remains a recorded paper-color outlier. These measurements
are color distances, not whole-card accuracy scores. See the
[color audit](../../games/magic/docs/hsb-calibration.md) for exact coverage and limits.

Set-symbol placement profiles remain unchanged in this release. Remaining symbol
material and positioning reviews continue separately. External references and
the review application are not shipped with the library.
