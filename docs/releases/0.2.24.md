# card-glint 0.2.24

Revised lands now use a fitted Fourth-family texture with colored pinlines,
striped dual-land paper and shared printing typography.

- Fit the art opening and paper box, crop the outer frame and draw an inset
  dark outline within the white border.
- Restore all ten dual-land band palettes while retaining the source texture.
  Basic-land paper colors and frame color calibration remain independently
  opt-in; original and Oracle wording use the same semantic land palette.
- Align the title, type, rules and illustrator credit across all 25 Revised
  land printings. Restore `Illus. ©` before the artist name.
- Cache each prepared material by source, palette, dimensions and calibration
  option. No material readback or recoloring occurs in the animation loop.

The fixed-scan comparison improves mean whole-card structural match from 40.9%
to 75.7%; twelve held-out printings improve from 41.8% to 75.1%. Rules overlap
improves from 20.5% to 75.9%. These are similarity indices, not percentages of
correctness. A larger basic-land text-size proposal was rejected after validation.
Existing texture and source-art color differences remain review items.

Other sets retain their existing layouts. Regenerate scores with the new renderer;
prior-version captures remain historical evidence. The public showcase continues
to load only original repository assets.
