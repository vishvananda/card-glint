# card-glint 0.6.7

Unlimited now has a complete, editable printing profile: frame placement and
materials, separate frame/paper color and black levels, historical mana geometry
and pigment, filled gray headings and shadows, text tiers, artist credit and
power/toughness. Basics and dual lands retain their original labels and measured
color/band treatments. Profile selection does not add per-scan renderer offsets.

All 302 historical Original controls match the measured candidate. The final
302 Originals add thirteen scan-reviewed artist spellings; every change is
confined to its artist span, with unchanged body and etching pixels. A further
604 Oracle faces verify modern and historical symbol choices with complete
wording, decoded assets and no text overflow. The full-card
structural index improves from 61.49 to 70.76/100: 278 cards improve and 24 regress.
Rules improve from 48.93 to 61.54/100, mana from 73.85 to 82.92/100.
Credits/legal are excluded from this structural aggregate; separate regional,
metadata and native pixel-isolation evidence validates the artist fixes. These are
similarity indices, not percentages of correctness. Residual wrapping and source
texture differences remain in the review evidence; every card stays in the report.

The profile editor preserves reflow inputs through early text-tier adjustments.
Zero edits preserve pixels, while physical margin and spacing edits retain the
selected horizontal scale and flavor spacing. Historical receipts remain bound
to their original data; installing a new profile cannot inherit old score approval.
Numerical evidence and raster hashes live in the Magic fixtures. External scan,
frame and font pixels remain outside Git and the public showcase.

This patch is compatible with existing consumers. Full M21 calibration is next,
including its land, legendary and planeswalker variants, before promoting modern
frame defaults.
