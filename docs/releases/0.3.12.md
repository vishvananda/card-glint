# card-glint 0.3.12

Revised nonland frames now use shared, material-specific placements of the
Fourth Edition source assets. The art and rules-box edges better match the
printed interior. Existing source-local color corrections and masks remain
bound to the exact assets; this release does not introduce another color fit.

All 306 Revised cards were recaptured against 0.3.11. The 279 nonlands with
accepted scan alignment improve in the whole-raster edge diagnostic, while
25 lands remain pixel-identical. Two uncertain scans remain excluded. The mean
one-pixel edge F1 rises from 60.66 to 75.03 / 100. This diagnostic is not the
component-weighted accuracy score or a percentage of flawless pixels.

All 42 Alpha/Beta/Unlimited controls and all 165 Planar Chaos printings remain
pixel-identical to 0.3.11. A broader Planar dense-text fitting experiment was
rejected after five regressions; its evidence remains available for outlier
review without changing the released typography.

The comparison integration reproduces all 306 frozen Revised interior scan
poses using isolated printed-texture strips, independently of renderer content.
Fit, model selection and certification use separate bands. These transforms
normalize comparison scans only; they do not alter rendered cards.

This patch changes no public interfaces or animated rendering work. Strict
Clippy, formatting, package/browser/showcase checks, the Magic accuracy suite,
and 513 distinct native face captures validate the renderer integration.
