# card-glint 0.2.11

The Magic pack uses complete styled set-symbol vectors, preserving their printed
white details, outlines and rarity gradients. Measured placement profiles adjust
position, width and height by set, frame family and border stock. Custom symbols
and the explicit Keyrune option retain their own behavior.

- Add source-pinned Eighth Edition and M15 replacement frame options, Matrix
  headers, and corrected dual-land color order and paper blending.
- Repair seven upstream SVG material definitions with checksum-guarded local
  patches. Prepared vectors share bounded cached leases across cards and tiers.
- Audit the major-set inventory with frozen frame-only scan normalization,
  reserved card names, CPU placement search and fresh Canvas validation. Adopt
  219 symbol profiles across 157 sets and 17 component-frame insets that pass
  those checks, from 6,321 sampled presentations across 272 major sets.
- Keep scan translation, scale, rotation and skew out of runtime card geometry.
  Record uncertain fits, missing sources and remaining asset-shape differences
  in the [calibration report](../../games/magic/docs/calibration-rollout.md).
- Expose color-profile coverage to hosts. The Revised audit finds that a single
  HSB multiplier cannot match both outer frame and paper on several materials;
  rejected profiles remain disabled.

Replacement frame families remain explicit options for ordinary faces. The
calibration report describes sampled coverage, not accuracy scores for every
card or support for special treatments. Source links and fan-content terms are
in the [Magic pack](../../games/magic/README.md). Downloaded card scans, artwork
and the review application are not included in the release.
