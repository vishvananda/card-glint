# card-glint 0.4.19

The Dark's crescent expansion symbol now uses measured width, height and
placement, replacing its oversized white backing with filled printing ink.
Black frames retain a subtle gray contour prepared from the same pinned SVG.
Public interfaces are unchanged. SVG variants share the source fetch and
decoded-image cache; painting does no additional decoding or mask readback.

The fit uses original printing inputs and frozen scan-only alignments. All 122
native faces match the candidates exactly, with no changes outside the symbol
region. Another 194 corpus faces and 84 unrelated printing controls remain
identical. Historical calibration evidence retains its measured source ancestry.

On the repeatedly inspected validation group, material-balanced symbol edge
overlap rises from 54.33 to 85.88 / 100; filled-area similarity rises from 45.19
to 80.68 / 100. Local contrast and aggregate stroke-width error also improve.
These are regional similarity indices, not whole-card scores or percentages of
correctness. Shared ink reduces the measured interior color error on all 120
geometry-qualified printings.

119 of those 120 cards improve both crops on edge, fill and contrast. Water
Wurm remains a regression: its crescent is farther left/larger than the shared
fit. Gaea's Touch 77† retains its shifted-ink exclusion, and Ball Lightning
retains its scan-pose rejection. Neither receives a per-card rendering offset.
Black symbols still have a small stroke-width mismatch despite their other
improvements. Type/body layout, measured gold mana transfer and complete-set
certification remain open. No scores were uploaded to hosted charts.
