# card-glint 0.4.3

This patch fills missing M21 outer-border color coverage and improves the
calibration checklist's scan-alignment evidence. Public interfaces are unchanged.

- Apply the existing source-bound M21 stock color to 33 ordinary lands and
  12 two-color cards. Pigment, mana settings and geometry stay unchanged.
  All 292 nonfoil M21 cards and 13 legendary lands from other sets were replayed:
  45 change and 260 remain pixel-identical. Central art, paper and alpha are
  preserved; translucent component edges can change by one RGB byte.
- Verify the synthetic M21-style legendary-land composition path separately.
  M21 itself contains no legendary lands. These controls do not certify crown
  geometry or colors, and actual legendary lands from other sets remain unchanged.
- Refine scan-only registration with cubic edge interpolation and finer fitting.
  All 80 known translation, scale, rotation and skew cases pass, including the
  earlier red and dual-land failures. The worst residual is 0.22 canonical pixels.
  Frozen mono-color templates and acceptance thresholds remain unchanged.
- Record 231 accepted poses across 239 mono-color and land scans. Eight failed
  scans remain visible. Normalization data is local comparison evidence; it does
  not change renderer placement or automatically update a hosted review service.
- Extend the completion ledger with the new coverage and preserve historical
  evidence through an explicit compatibility check. Outer stock remains a partial
  black-level calibration until interior ink has been measured too.

On the 26 accepted land references, median error across three flat border-color
samples improves from 10.67 to 0.00 RGB byte units. That regional statistic does
not measure texture or whole-card accuracy. References are reused development
data; land paper, crowns, typography, special layouts and full-card scores remain
open. No set is claimed fully calibrated.

Validation includes native pixel replay, 645 selector controls, strict native
and WASM Clippy, rustfmt, the Magic accuracy suite, package consumption, browser
tests and the local-only showcase. The public showcase uses only repository assets.
