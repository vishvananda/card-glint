Refined optical treatments and touch interaction.

- Rain uses smaller refractive beads, light fog, draining drops and clear trails.
- Heat moves mainly horizontally, with slight image softening.
- Pool caustics come from traveling waves and approximate light convergence,
  replacing the anchored cellular pattern.
- All optical treatments respond to card tilt.
- Touch dragging tilts cards; swiping beside or between them scrolls the page.
- The showcase's Full card / Artwork only toggle applies to all treatments and
  PNG exports, preserving over-finish overlays and cached faces/etching.

The card outline remains fixed in this release. Optical effects distort its
printed contents. [References and limits](../optical-surfaces.md) describe the
approximations and fragment costs.
