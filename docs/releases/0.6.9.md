# card-glint 0.6.9

Unlimited dual lands use a shared neutral-background correction measured from
nine scans. The unusually dark Volcanic Island scan is excluded from that fit
and remains in the score report. Badlands, Bayou and Underground Sea have a
separate charcoal correction on their outer colored strip, preserving the
inner bands around their art and rules boxes. Existing assets are retained.

All 302 Originals and 604 Oracle faces were replayed. The 292 non-dual printings,
all text layouts, etchings, illustration interiors and silhouettes are unchanged.
The neutral-background color diagnostic improves, as do all three black outlines.
The structural index changes from 70.974 to 70.961/100; the ten-dual mean changes
from 70.439 to 70.061/100. The report retains these contrast-sensitive regressions
rather than hiding the dark scan or using edge overlap to choose pigment colors.

No public API changes. Unlimited editor proposals must be rebased from profile
revision 2 to revision 3. Historical calibration and release receipts remain
immutable. External scans, art and fonts are not shipped in the public showcase.
