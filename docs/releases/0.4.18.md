# card-glint 0.4.18

The Dark's four nonbasic lands and three gold cards now use the set's calibrated
title and artist lettering. Both gold creatures also use its calibrated
power/toughness typography. These are the existing shared values, transferred
without fitting new per-card or per-material settings. Public interfaces are
unchanged.

All seven cards improve against the same original printing scans and frozen
frame poses. The local whole-card structural index rises from 69.85 to 72.55;
with independently reviewed credits included, it rises from 70.38 to 73.01.
These are similarity indices, not percentages of correctness or hosted scores.
Filled-area and local-contrast checks accompany edge overlap. Land lettering
still has a stroke-width mismatch despite the aggregate improvement, and
City of Shadows loses some title-contrast similarity.

All seven native renders match the reviewed transfer exactly. The other 309
early-expansion faces and 84 unrelated printing controls remain pixel-identical.
Frame, art, symbols, type line and body pixels are unchanged. Historical
calibration measurements retain their original source ancestry.

[Numeric evidence](../../games/magic/fixtures/accuracy/early-expansion-dark-typography.json)
records the component isolation, transfer measurements and native replay.
Type-line and body layout, expansion-symbol rim/placement, mana transfer
measurements and complete-set certification remain open.
