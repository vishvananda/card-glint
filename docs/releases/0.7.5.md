# card-glint 0.7.5

This patch adds independently measured Mirage and Onslaught profiles and better
starting defaults for 34 other ordinary old-frame sets. Existing APIs remain
compatible.

- Calibrate frame geometry, frame and paper colors, black levels, historical mana,
  expansion symbols, headings, rules and flavor text, illustrator credits,
  copyright and power/toughness.
- Install reviewed original type lines and artist credits for all 701 nonfoil
  Mirage and Onslaught printings. Preserve Oracle wording separately.
- Use the best measured geometry among Fourth Edition, Mirage and Onslaught for
  each other old-frame set. Retain target surrounds, symbols, footer and stats,
  and keep existing heading, mana or rules components when their pilot fit wins.
- Select colors separately from geometry, including Onslaught palettes for
  Legions and Scourge and measured Seventh Edition pigment supplements.
- Recover seven previously rejected scan alignments using frame-only RGB
  gradients and fixed smoothing. Retain all 694 previously accepted transforms.
  No renderer text or artwork participates in scan alignment.
- Correct Mirage gold copyright ink to match the light text in its scans.
- Repair Arabian Nights and Antiquities artifact sources, removing baked-in
  edge strips and measuring their frame and paper colors separately.

Mirage's complete native structural mean is 75.38/100; Onslaught's is
76.48/100. These indices measure structural agreement, not percentages of correct
pixels. The shared-default pilot covers 778 native faces and 526 accepted scans;
rejected references remain listed.

The complete nonfoil census uses original printing wording, cached references
and native renders. These scans are reused development data, not an independent
holdout. One foil-only Onslaught alternate printing remains explicitly queued;
foil scans do not determine base frame colors. Shared defaults are starting
points for later audits and do not certify those 34 sets as complete.

The evidence records per-card source, native raster, scan transform and metric
identities. Scores are reused only when those inputs agree. Artist and copyright
are reviewed separately because the structural metric excludes credits.
No external card art is added to the public showcase.
