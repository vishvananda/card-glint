# card-glint 0.2.27

Eighth Edition basic lands now fit their printed inner frames and palettes more
closely. One shared geometry correction expands the inner plate and its artwork
aperture while preserving the outer stock and rounded corners.

With `colorCalibration: 'set-hsb-v1'`, source-bound masks separate the brown outer
texture, colored pinlines, name/type inserts and rules paper. A shared tone curve
reduces excessive texture contrast. Large mana glyph fills receive independent
color targets while preserving neutral shadows and white highlights. Prepared
materials reuse the existing bounded cache; warm preparation adds no readbacks.

Nineteen aligned nonfoil printings improve from 67.5% to 75.9% mean structural
similarity. Nine reserved printings improve from 68.5% to 75.6%. Their median
pinline color distance falls from 5.06 to 0.68, and outer-texture contrast error
from 8.31 to 2.26. These are similarity/color diagnostics, not percentages of
correct pixels. Swamp 339 retains its explicit alignment exclusion.

Color-only changes can affect the contrast-threshold structural metric. One
reserved Island loses 3.1 structural points relative to geometry alone while
remaining 7.3 points above 0.2.26; color evidence is reported independently.
Source texture, header typography and exact printed legal text remain review
items. Other sets, custom palettes and black-border treatments retain their
existing profiles. Regenerate scores for the new renderer version.
