# card-glint 0.5.4

Alpha and Beta receive full-set text and printing-color checks against original
wording, with scan alignment kept separate from rendering defaults. Shared size
rules use passage length and measured wrapping; printing exceptions remain
content matched. Oracle wording keeps its independent fitting policy.

Historical cost geometry and mana-disc/ink colors are reviewed separately.
Original Drain Life uses its printed cost; Oracle keeps its current cost.
Experimental reconstructed Alpha frames remain preview-only.

Gloss now reflects a fixed, softly blurred studio light as the card tilts.
The effect remains subtle and works with cardstock. Public APIs are unchanged;
card motion remains controlled by the host application.
