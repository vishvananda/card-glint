# card-glint 0.2.22

Improve ordinary Alpha, Beta, Revised, Eighth Edition and M21 printings using
independently normalized scans and shared printing defaults.

- Fit title translations against frozen frame poses. Alpha, Beta, Eighth and
  M21 receive measured offsets; Revised retains its existing placement because
  a translation does not improve its reserved examples.
- Adopt the reviewed Beta mana cost spacing, numeral position and per-color
  glyph adjustments, including the centered historical white sun. Separate
  each glyph from its circle and preserve editor deltas from the new defaults.
- Move Eighth Edition's mana row up two canonical pixels and fit its circles,
  numerals and glyphs. All 48 reserved cards improve. Retain Alpha, Revised and
  M21 mana geometry where proposed replacements fail the reserved-card gates.
- Add thirteen reserved-scan mana color targets for Alpha, Revised, Eighth and
  M21, alongside the six reviewed Beta targets. Keep previous colors where a
  proposed change regresses reserved cards. Frame/paper HSB remains opt-in and
  guarded by exact source assets and geometry; its coverage remains partial.
- Restore original type labels for 39 early dual lands, 55 basic lands and
  four Dwarven Warriors printings. Preserve semantic frame palettes when the
  original type is simply `Land`; Oracle wording remains available separately.
- Compose Alpha/Beta/Unlimited dual-land frame bands from the pinned colored
  land plates and alpha mask. Cache that composition separately from HSB
  materials. New composite sources do not inherit neutral-land color fits.
- Expand compact original-text costs such as `{2RRR}` into separate symbols,
  fixing thirteen Eighth Edition printings that could not render. Hybrid,
  Phyrexian and multi-digit generic symbols remain intact.
- Correct the rotated Aftermath type-line rectangle and its semantic hit region,
  moving the lower type label right and up on the upright card.

Per-card scan transforms are comparison inputs only; they never become card
rendering offsets. Failed frame matches remain review exclusions. ABU dual-land
color fitting, Revised's striped text-box paper, remaining title font-shape
mismatches, and unresolved frame-template failures remain follow-up work.
Regenerate stored scores for this release; do not relabel earlier captures.
The public showcase continues to load only original repository assets.
