# card-glint 0.2.28

Ordinary modern cards and planeswalkers now draw their oval security stamps,
using separate colored mounts for the two frame families. Physical printing
metadata decides whether a stamp is present; older rares and unstamped uncommons
remain unstamped. Original and Oracle wording keep the same physical mark.

- Add `securityStamp` to the Magic input. Explicit `oval` and `none` override
  rarity; omitted custom modern rare/mythic inputs use the oval default.
- Honor the same metadata in Saga, Adventure, Prepare, Aftermath and Split/Fuse.
  Split/Fuse retains its plain oval, and double-sided backs do not inherit the
  front stamp. Other stamp shapes remain metadata only.
- Reserve ordinary text clearance around the mount. Normalize the native mount's
  black foot to the card stock in the bounded material cache; warm preparation
  and animation add no pixel readbacks.

Twenty-four frame-aligned examples have mean oval-center differences of 0.48 px
horizontally and 0.61 px vertically at 488 × 680. Source-native bounds are retained.
Five scan-alignment failures remain explicit exclusions. Two unstamped controls
are pixel-identical. Fourteen ordinary examples improve mean structural similarity
from 67.4% to 70.8%; six reserved examples improve from 70.0% to 71.2%. Four individual
scores fall, with Vito's flavor wrapping the largest regression at 0.9 points.
These are finite-cohort similarity indices, not percentages of correctness.

The hologram is static in this release. Its pattern and reflected colors differ
from the scan; the localized animated effect remains a separate follow-up.
See the Magic pack's security-stamp fixture for sources, frozen poses and results.

Full-set verification covers 1,543 printings across Alpha, Beta, Revised, Eighth
and M21: 1,517 scored, 26 explicit alignment exclusions, no capture failures.
All scan transforms and normalized references match the previous release. M21
mean structural match rises from 70.94% to 71.23%. The wider run also exposed
dense-text regressions on Liliana’s Scorn, Terror of the Peaks and Volcanic Salvo:
stamp clearance reduces their type size and changes wrapping. These remain
visible in the reported averages and queued for text calibration. A generic
upward-clearance alternative regressed the broader cohort and was not released.
