# card-glint 0.6.4

Planeswalkers use calibrated Card Conjurer backgrounds across all seven palettes,
with the reviewed frame geometry and an inner type-box bezel.

- Move the art bottom, type box and ability-box top up 4 reference pixels together.
  Keep the name box and ability-box bottom fixed. Pull the right surround, art
  aperture and ability-paper contour inward 2 pixels, retaining original plate widths.
- Replace the type-box outer drop shadow with a clipped dark inner bezel. Retain
  the measured upper-right curve, left flare and paper texture.
- Widen name ink by 6% and type ink by 3% where space permits. Crowded type
  lines retain their existing width; do not shrink their font just to widen them.
- Measure symbol width and height independently at a fixed lower-right anchor.
  Source-specific values cover M15, M19–M21, WAR, C14, DOM, SOI and DFT.
  Other sources and host-supplied symbols keep their original sizing.
- Remove the added black exterior stroke from M15 uncommon/rare/mythic symbols,
  retaining black lettering and inset details. Common symbols are unchanged.
- Derived background and frame images share an atomic, bounded 64 MiB cache;
  document/worker composition share the same pixels. No preparation runs during tilt.

Fits use original MTGJSON wording and frozen exterior-only scan registration,
including scale, rotation and shear. 39 scans fit shared values and 20 are reserved
for validation; two failed registrations and one missing original-wording input
remain explicit. Measurements and raster hashes are in the Magic fixtures.
Regional ink distances and RGB errors are diagnostics, not whole-card compliance.

This patch requires no consumer API changes. Changed planeswalkers and M15
symbols get new face identities; unrelated faces retain their keys and evidence.
The earlier 0.6.3 candidate was withheld after visual review; 0.6.4 includes its
background improvements and the subsequent geometry corrections. No downloaded
reference pixels are included in the package or public showcase.
