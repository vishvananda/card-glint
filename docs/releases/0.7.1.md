# card-glint 0.7.1

Updates the audited Alpha, Beta and Unlimited baseline using all 899 ordinary
Original printings and the existing frame assets. This patch does not change
the public API.

Original text fitting uses fractional advances without inflating the legacy
row budget. Small glyph widths, short keyword blocks, and the reviewed
Word of Command and Howl from Beyond layouts now follow the printed passages
more closely. Oracle rendering keeps its own wording and fitting policy.

Alpha/Beta dual lands receive the measured perimeter, paper-panel and art
aperture corrections. Reviewed Original corrections include Stasis wording,
Pearled Unicorn's attribution break, and Alpha Tropical Island's artist credit.
Field-only wording reviews remain separate from whole-body approval.

These are finite source-census results on reused development references with
frozen scan alignment. They are similarity indices, not percentages or blind
validation. The final body update rescored 33 changed images and reused 866
exact image/reference/pose/input/metric identities. All negative deltas remain
included; Unlimited Stasis keeps its correct printed wording despite a lower
structural index.

The audit retains manual wrapping, punctuation and source-texture residuals,
including Beta Clone and Alpha Savannah. Native checks cover all 899 faces and
376 Original/Oracle layer pairs. Publication and matching Lab score rollout
remain separate from a component fit.

The experimental Alpha frame study must be refreshed with the matching
renderer, both rebuild columns, aperture masks, scores and versioned asset URLs.
Experimental reconstructed frames remain outside the library package.
