# card-glint 0.5.5

Original Alpha wording now preserves the unprinted cost on Cyclopean Tomb and
the printed 1R costs on Orcish Artillery and Orcish Oriflamme. Oracle costs and
Beta remain unchanged. The exhaustive cost review covers all 597 Alpha and Beta
printings; only the three necessary corrections enter browser payloads.

Mana audits verify disc size, placement, spacing, independent width/height,
per-color glyphs, generic numerals, paper and ink. The study now prepares numeral
controls even when defaults are unadjusted, rejects controls that change no
pixels, and checks active trials against full native renders. Small proposed
changes that did not reproduce across broader cohorts were retained as rejected
evidence. Existing rounded corners and reconstructed-frame previews are unchanged.

No public interfaces changed.
