# card-glint 0.3.2

Ordinary modern Magic frames now share improved mana-cost numerals, disc sizing
and shadows. The defaults follow the component frame across sets, including
ordinary legendary cards. Existing per-card adjustment controls remain relative
to these defaults. Public interfaces are unchanged.

Single-digit generic costs use the pinned Card Conjurer numeral artwork. Discs
are 1% larger, numerals are 2% smaller and move 0.25 px left, and the shadow moves
0.5 px right and 0.5 px down. Inter-symbol spacing is unchanged. Inline symbols,
old frames, Eighth Edition plates and special layouts retain their own settings.
Resetting the numeral transform to identity retains the selected numeral source.

The M21 fit improved mana overlap from 89.61% to 92.89% on 41 held-out cards.
A separate, frozen-settings transfer study accepted 148 cards across four sets:
M15 improved from 59.75% to 64.34%, WAR from 70.15% to 73.75%, and Duskmourn from
51.35% to 54.36%; DMU declined from 83.77% to 82.93%. These are structural overlap
indices, not percentages of correct pixels. Source-shape diagnostics improved
in all four sets, but DMU and Duskmourn retain placement warnings. The shared
values are a better starting point, not a claim of finished calibration in every
set. Subsequent set overrides can refine them without changing consumer code.

All 148 final integration rasters match their frozen experimental candidates
exactly. Scan poses and source assets were held fixed; no per-card mana refits
were used to improve the transfer scores. Evidence, exclusions and individual
regressions are retained in the Magic pack's accuracy fixtures.

The 0.3.1 tag did not publish: the standalone archive smoke test used a flat
placeholder without separable mana ink. Its synthetic fixture now supplies an
opaque disc and glyph, and reports preparation errors directly. Renderer
validation is unchanged.
