# card-glint 0.4.12

This patch corrects early-expansion titles to solid light-gray lettering with
thinner strokes and a lower-right shadow. It changes no public interfaces or
font downloads. The incorrect hollow treatment in 0.4.11 was withdrawn before
publication; that tag is not reused.

Shared title profiles cover ordinary Arabian Nights, Antiquities and The Dark
printings. Original names and frozen scan poses drive calibration. The Dark's
unresolved land/gold plates and custom frame choices keep their prior treatment.

All 316 native renders reproduce the prepared candidates exactly. The 306
qualified title previews match their full-card pixels exactly, and no pixels
outside the title area change. Eighty-four unrelated cards across twelve sets
retain identical PNGs. Ten unresolved scans remain excluded from scoring.

Repeated validation covers 206 printings excluded from parameter fitting. These
scans were inspected in prior studies and are not a fresh holdout. Independent
title-region edge overlap in the primary crop changes as follows:

| Set            | Before | After | Improved validation printings |
| -------------- | -----: | ----: | ----------------------------: |
| Arabian Nights |  52.7% | 70.3% |                       51 / 51 |
| Antiquities    |  49.6% | 64.0% |                       67 / 71 |
| The Dark       |  51.0% | 73.7% |                       84 / 84 |

Three fixed comparison crops pass the recorded adoption gates. Four Antiquities
validation printings regress in the primary and lettering-bounded crops, by at
most 1.84 percentage points across those crops. They remain in the report.

The unchanged whole-card structural index improves from 70.15 to 71.82 for ARN,
69.11 to 70.58 for ATQ, and 70.02 to 71.98 for DRK (out of 100). Of 306 qualified
printings, 302 improve; four ATQ printings regress by at most 0.18 points. These
indices are similarity diagnostics, not correctness percentages. They retain
the existing legal-credit exclusion and do not update hosted score charts.

The calibration tools now include locally normalized contrast and filled-area
checks, plus a stroke-width proxy. Synthetic tests distinguish filled strokes
from sharper hollow outlines despite brightness, contrast and noise changes.
Real scans expose an important limit: the stroke-width proxy still favors the
rejected ARN/ATQ treatment on average. It remains a diagnostic, not an adoption
gate. Historical ink style and color must be established separately from
geometry fitting.

A review of text-painting paths and 37 representative scan comparisons is
recorded in `games/magic/fixtures/accuracy/lettering-style-audit.json`. The
remaining hollow early-expansion artist profiles are marked partial pending
an ink-model refit. ABU and Legends lettering also has weight-review follow-ups.
Those existing renderers are unchanged by this patch.
